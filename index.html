Who We Are

Two fields, one company: HDD infrastructure and smart software

Cakir IT-Solutions is built on real HDD field experience since 2016: horizontal drilling, pipe and cable installation, street lighting, electrical connections and civil works across Belgium, the Netherlands and Germany.

On top of that, the company develops IT and software tools: logistics dashboards, planning systems, smart display concepts and multilingual interfaces for infrastructure and business environments.

This combination makes the company both practical and modern: one team that understands the ground and the screen.
