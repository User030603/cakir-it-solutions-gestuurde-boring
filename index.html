Who We Are

Field work and IT in one company

The HDD side of Cakir Technics is built on real site work since 2016: horizontal drilling for ducts and pipes, pulling power and data cables, placing foundations and lantern poles, making connections and restoring roads and pavements after the works.

The IT-Solutions side builds tools around this reality: dashboards to follow routes and jobs, planning overviews for crews, simple web and mobile screens and smart displays for status, safety and customer information.

This way the same company prepares the trench, installs the cable and also shows the work clearly on screen.
